Overview & General Requirements

Protocols and expectations for outside-only SRO customer visits where all work is outside the home and does not require customer interaction.

Safety First

If you encounter any situation or interaction where you feel unsafe, immediately remove yourself and contact your supervisor. All current safety processes and procedures must be followed.

Standards & Deviations

  • Steps outlined in this document are the standard and may not be modified.
  • Any deviations must be approved by Corporate Technical Operations.
  • Existing OES materials govern drop networks and CPE.

Workmanship & Work Area

  • All work performed will be of the highest possible workmanship.
  • Must adhere to local, state, federal codes, and landlord stipulations.
  • Work area must be kept clean, orderly, and free of trash/debris.

Approved Materials

Only products approved by Corporate Engineering shall be used in the RF and optical drop system. Optical passives must meet NGAN or XNET guidelines.

Preparation & Arrival

Pre-Job Preparation

  • Review PHT (Premise Health Test) results if available.
  • Review job notes and expectations.
  • Follow other key elements defined in ULearn training and OES.

Arrival

  • Perform the Circle of Safety.
  • Check personal appearance.
  • Conduct a jobsite survey.

Customer Awareness

  • Attempt to contact the customer. (If unreachable, try again at wrap-up).
  • Set clear expectations.
  • Inform them work is outside only and if service interruption is possible.

Outside Drop System Validation

All work is to begin at the bondblock/service distribution point.

1. Visual Inspection & Safety

  • Use Foreign Voltage Detector (FVD) to ensure no voltage present. (If present, follow foreign voltage safety procedures).
  • Utilize amp clamp to ensure current is below threshold (< 1 amp).

2. Bonding Validation

  • Validate bonding meets OES requirements (Bondblock, ground wire, electrical connection).
  • Perform ohm test to ensure bonding is < 25 ohms.
  • Utilize Bonding Validation Tool (BVT) application.
  • Verify POE is present or installed per MoCA standard.

3. Signal Testing (At Bondblock)

If drop is active, proceed. If not, terminate drop and move to tap. All signal testing uses the XM Meter platform (save results to cloud).

  • DS Spectrum Test: Verify forward levels are within OES thresholds.
  • DOCSIS Test: Verify US and DS levels are within OES RF thresholds.
  • Complete any other testing required by Division Tech Ops.

Tap Procedures

Inspection at the Tap

  • Follow all safety procedures. Use FVD to ensure no voltage.
  • Ensure all cables, connectors, fasteners, and drop components follow OES and are approved by Corporate Engineering.

Signal Level Testing

  • DS Spectrum & DOCSIS Test: Verify levels within thresholds.
  • Ingress Test: Scan drop; must meet XM Ingress compliance. Any spike over -30dB must be corrected.
  • Perform Leakage or other required testing.

Certify, Repair, Replace, or RTM

Based on inspection and testing results:

  • Certify the drop is within specification.
  • Replace the drop if it does not meet minimum specifications.
  • Submit an RTM if levels from the tap are not within spec (follow OES Fulfillment RTM).

After tap certification, return to the bondblock to complete testing.

Exterior Network & Wrap-up

Home Network Distribution - Exterior

  • Visual Inspection: Inspect all active cables on the outside of the home.
  • Ingress Test: Perform scan on home drop system. Any spike over -30dB must be corrected or mitigated.
  • If ingress cannot be corrected from outside, contact supervisor and follow division/regional processes.
  • Validate, repair, or replace as needed.

Job Wrap-up

  • Ensure cable routing, fasteners, and materials follow OES requirements.
  • Verify the reason for the SRO is resolved (e.g., leakage repaired).
  • Verify services restored using PHT to ensure equipment is reporting and in spec.

Customer Awareness

  • Attempt contact again.
  • Verify restoration, apologize for inconvenience, and thank them.

Complete the Job

  • Verify tools/equipment returned to vehicle.
  • Ensure follow-ups (One Drop, RTM) are complete.
  • Utilize proper coding to close. Complete Circle of Safety.
