General Products and Supplies

  • Only products approved by Comcast Corporate Engineering shall be used in the RF, Category/twisted pair, and/or optical wiring system.
  • All optical passive and active devices must meet Comcast NGAN Corporate Engineering guidelines.

Bandwidth Frequency Ranges

RF Bandwidth

  • 54 MHz to 1.2 GHz (Sub-split): Minimum return RF bandwidth of 5-42 MHz. Must accommodate MoCA up to 1.2 GHz.
  • 85 MHz to 1.2 GHz (Mid-split): Minimum forward RF 85 MHz-1.2 GHz. Minimum return 5-85 MHz. Must accommodate MoCA up to 1.2 GHz.
  • 108 MHz to 684 MHz (FDX): Forward RF 108 MHz-1.2 GHz. Full duplex bandwidth 108-684 MHz. Return path 5-85 MHz.

Ethernet Bandwidth

  • Cat5e: Minimum 1 MHz to 100 MHz.
  • Cat6: 1 MHz to 550 MHz.

Optical Bandwidth

  • Drop fiber must pass all required optical wavelengths (CWDM/DWDM).
  • Single mode fiber: Used for the drop. Wavelengths passed: 1260 nm to 1625 nm.
  • Optical wavelength filtering used in RFoG and EPON networks.

Cable Type & Specifications

RF Cables

Must meet ANSI/SCTE 74 2011.

Minimum size of Series 6 with foil-braid-foil, tri-shield configuration (bonded inner foil, outer foil bonded to jacket, at least 77% braid coverage).

Unshielded Network (UTP)

Must meet ANSI/TIA/EIA-568-B.

All new installations must be a minimum of Category 6 (Cat6).

Shielded Network (STP)

Used in high ingress potential environments / OSP.

Minimum Category 6. Must meet ANSI/TIA/EIA-568-B, B2, B3.

Fiber Optic Cable

Must meet ANSI/ICEA S-110-717-2013.

Must be pushable, flat, or round depending on drop. Must have a CIFA number.

Plenum & Riser Cables

Must meet fire safety test standards in accordance with NFPA 262 and outlined in NFPA 90A.

Connectors & Weatherproofing

F Connectors

  • Prep drop cable to 1/4" x 1/4" dimensions for Series 6.
  • Must meet SCTE 123 2011 or SCTE 124 2021 specifications.
  • Must be compression-type with 360-degree retention of the cable.
  • All new outdoor F connectors must have a thread protector attached or pre-installed.

Tightening Specifications

  • Drop system components (splitters, traps, terminators, wall plates) tightened to 30 inch-pounds using a torque-limiting wrench.
  • F connectors on CPE tightened not to exceed 10 inch-pounds (except when using certified prepackaged quick-tight jumpers).

Weatherproofing

  • Protect outdoor interfaces to prevent moisture contamination.
  • Never use silicone compounds or heat-shrinkable tubing on drop cable/F connectors.
  • Thread protectors must not be used on indoor connectors or CPE.

Fiber Optic Connectors

SC/APC & LC/APC (Green)

8-degree angle polish. Used for analog optical solutions (RFoG) requiring high Optical Return Loss (ORL). LC/APC used in MetroE.

SC/UPC (Blue)

Polished flat end face across the core. Digital applications work well using UPC. Used in EPON installations.

Connector Prep Tools

RF Cable Tools

  • Fixed blade prep tool (no field adjustments).
  • Replace cutting blades monthly.
  • Must have a notch to prevent nicking the center conductor.
  • Compression tools must match connector style and be inspected monthly.

Network Tools

  • Must meet ANSI/TIA-568 specs for cable and connector type.
  • Feed-through connectors are permitted.

Optical Fiber

  • Splicing/connectorization performed by qualified personnel only.
  • RFoG and Metro E must be fusion spliced.
  • EPON can be fusion, mechanical, or field installable.

Hardware for Connections

Filters

  • Must be weatherproofed with correct size thread protection on both ends.
  • If no pre-installed protector exists, use a separate one for the 0.500" port length.
  • In aerial installations with jumpers, filters must be attached to the strand or hanger bracket (no strain on drop, maintain bend radius).

RF Port Termination

  • All unused ports must be terminated using 75-ohm resistors.
  • Terminators on tap ports shall have a nonconductive port saver + thread protection.

Barrel Connectors (F-81)

  • Outdoor: Must meet ANSI/SCTE 01 2015. Requires non-threaded area at base for weatherproofing.
  • Indoor: Must meet ANSI/SCTE 02 2015. Threads all the way to base for panel nuts. Never use outdoors.

Bond Blocks

  • Must meet ANSI/SCTE 129 2017.
  • Must accommodate wire sizes from #10 AWG to #12 AWG.
  • Installation of MoCA ground block or PoE filter is mandatory (except FDX customers).

Drop Clamps & Fasteners

  • Drop clamps use only the messenger wire to support the drop (must not crush/deform coax).
  • Install clamps where tension is introduced (Tap, premise). Mid-span is optional.
  • No metal staples on coaxial cables.
  • Clip guns must use a half-round plastic insert.
  • Plastic fasteners must be UV and ozone stable.

Splitters, Couplers & Amps

  • Passive devices must meet ANSI/SCTE 153 2016. Drop amps meet ANSI/SCTE 161 2016.
  • All RF splitters and amplifiers must be MoCA compliant.
  • Amplification on the forward path must provide unity gain.
  • Optical couplers/splitters are not to be used after the demarcation enclosure.

Wall Plates & Jacks

  • Coaxial: Must have a 7/16-inch hex recess to hold the F-81 and prevent turning.
  • Network: Must meet ANSI/TIA-568 specs.
  • Optical: Must use an SC type bulkhead adapter with an auto-shutter/angled port to restrict laser light exposure.
