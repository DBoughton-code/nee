Operational Excellence Standard (OES) - Connected Devices Insights

Overview

The Connected Devices Insights feature in Tech360 enables the technician to view all devices connected to the customer's gateway, including interactive filters and in-depth telemetry to help prioritize triaging potentially impaired devices. It provides a snapshot of all devices on the customer's WiFi network provided by a Comcast device, as well as wired devices.

Note: Customer-owned (COAM) WiFi routers, Access Points, or mesh devices may not display.

Telemetry Features & Definitions

PHY Rates

Refers to physical communication speed between IP devices (in Mbps). Low rates can be normal if a device is in standby/asleep or not actively transferring data.

RSSI (Received Signal Strength Indicator)

A WiFi signal strength indicator. While a good measure of signal strength, it measures strength, not necessarily quality.

SNR (Signal to Noise Ratio)

Strength of a signal relative to background noise or interference. Higher SNR indicates a stronger, clearer signal with less noise interference.

Speed to Gateway

Speed of internet connection between the head end and gateway. A failure is defined as receiving less than 90% of advertised speed after a restart on two tests in the Xfinity app.

Gateway Info Make, model, and current firmware of the gateway.
SSID Service Set Identifier (broadcasting name of the wireless network).
Wireless Devices Client devices connected via IEEE 802.11 standards.
Wired Devices Client devices connected via IEEE 802.3 standards.

Technician Guidance

Where to Access

Available through Tech360 in the Account Health tab, under Connected devices.

When to Use

On all jobs where a customer reports slow speeds or intermittent connectivity that are not related to RF signal quality or modem/gateway performance issues.

Supplemental Workflow

Step 1 Troubleshooting

  • Perform normal troubleshooting processes to identify if RF impairments exist between the tap and gateway and validate that all OES requirements are met.
  • Use your XM Meter to verify correct speeds at the tap, bond block, and outlet.
  • If no RF impairments exist, run an XM Meter speed test at the gateway via Ethernet to verify device health.

Step 2 Perform Connected Devices Test

  • Once the test completes, review the devices identified as impaired.
  • Determine which impaired device(s) the customer is experiencing issues with.
  • Review the reported errors. Check recommended actions ("Check for the following" suggestions).
  • Review the proposed actions with the customer before making any changes.
  • After performing actions, refresh the connected devices test and review the new results with the customer.
