📝 Drilling & Penetration Consent

Customer Authorization & Instant PDF Generation

1. Authorization to Penetrate Surface: By signing below, the customer/property owner authorizes the technician to drill the agreed-upon surface hole(s) solely for the routing of telecommunication coaxial/fiber cabling required for service delivery.

2. Pre-Drill Inspection & Hidden Utilities: The customer acknowledges that the technician has visually inspected the interior and exterior surfaces. However, neither the contractor nor service provider shall be held liable for damage to concealed or unmapped plumbing, electrical wiring, HVAC ductwork, or insulation located within concealed cavities unless caused by gross negligence.

3. Weatherproofing Seal: The technician agrees to install an exterior wall bushing and seal the penetration with approved silicone/duct-seal compound.

4. Tenant Representation: If the signatory is a tenant, they confirm they have obtained explicit consent from the landlord/property owner.
