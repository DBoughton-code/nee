Overview & At a Glance

A Trouble Call (TC) is a job created in response to a customer-reported service issue, whether real or perceived. TCs may be new, repeat, chronic, or install-related.

Core Presumption & Rule: TCs are created with the presumption that the customer's services have been installed and working properly but now need repair. All TCs must be rolled upon and completed as scheduled.

Exceptions

All TCs must be rolled upon and completed as scheduled, with exceptions allowed only for the following scenarios:

Sanctioned "Scrubbing"

Specialized teams may contact customers to resolve issues faster than the scheduled TC. Call-aheads are only for authorized centers.

Failed Getting Started Kit

If a tech arrives and finds a GSK activation happened within the last 30 days, the TC should be canceled and replaced by a Tech Recovery job type.

Install-Related

If no service issues are present and the customer instead wants additional services (device/outlet changes), contact CommOps to cancel the TC and create a CoS order.

Not Home / No Access (Table 2-1)

Critical: TCs must not be rescheduled in cases where the customer is not home or the technician has no access. The job must be completed with the proper resolution code.

Code Short Description Use Case
U10 Not Home - Checked outside only Customer is not home, and technician was able to check outside connections at the premise.
U11 Customer Home - No Access Technician contacted customer but could not access a location/room, preventing resolution.
U12 Not Home and No Outside Check Customer not home, and technician unable to check/verify outside connections.
U14 Customer Refused Entry Customer cancels upon technician arrival and does not allow any work to be completed.
U21 Okay on Arrival After troubleshooting, technician determines customer has no service issues and passes PHT score.

Outages, CoS & LOB Errors

System Outage

If scheduled during an active outage or system issue (billing system) that impacts the job, the TC should be rescheduled with a system outage reschedule reason. The customer must be notified prior. Make every effort to reschedule for the same day.

Converting TC to CoS

If the technician determines services need to be added or installed (instead of repaired), the TC can be canceled by the TSC and the correct Change of Service (CoS) order keyed to generate appropriate installation fees and recurring charges.

Line of Business (LOB) Error

If the TC is for the wrong line of business (e.g., Commercial, SmartOffice) and the tech lacks the skill set, request CommOps for a same-day tech assist. If unavailable, complete to the best of the technician's capability and create a Special Request Order (SRO) for additional work.
