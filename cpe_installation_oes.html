Customer Premise Equipment (CPE)

At a Glance

This document covers the requirements for CPE installation to deliver the products and services that a customer has subscribed to. This standard provides guidelines for the installation of both customer-owned and Comcast-owned CPE. All CPE connected to Comcast's network must be approved by Comcast Corporate Engineering.

CPE Location Considerations

  • Do not place CPE in an enclosed area where the cooling vents are blocked or impede the flow of air.
  • Do not place CPE on a soft surface such as a sofa, rug, or similar surface that could block ventilation.
  • Install CPE away from heat sources such as radiators, heat registers, and stoves.
  • Installation near other electronic devices is permitted if the location provides adequate air circulation.
  • Place CPE on a flat surface not prone to vibration or impact.
  • Install inside a premise where condensation will not occur. Do not install near a water source (sink, bathtub, etc.).
  • Do not install CPE on a switched power outlet.

Limiting Potential Interference

  • Do not place devices with WiFi technology on top of or right next to other devices with WiFi technology. Radiating devices may impact each other's performance.
  • Keep all WiFi technology devices out of cabinets that have doors and out of shelving that is all metal.
  • Only place WiFi technology devices in media panels designed for WiFi. These are made of plastic, never metal.
  • Avoid placing WiFi technology devices next to mirrors, glass, and/or fish tanks.
  • Do not place devices with WiFi technology next to large appliances or microwaves.

CPE Installation Guidelines

Wiring & Connections

  • Use Comcast-approved cords and cables to complete all connections.
  • Verify that the correct power cord/adapter is used for the equipment (check the manufacturer's label on the device).
  • Ensure all RF connectors are tightened to specifications.

Safety & Aesthetics

  • Install all wiring in a neat and tidy fashion.
  • Ensure that cords are not crossing walkways or posing a tripping hazard.
  • Power cords must never be routed/fished through a wall or in a wall cavity.
  • Cords are not to be placed under rugs or carpeting.
  • Protect the cords from being pinched, particularly at plugs/wall plates and connection points.

When Wall Mounting CPE

  • Do not mount the device near HVAC outlets, radiators, or any other source of heat.
  • Do not mount the device near any object(s) that could restrict air flow to the device.
  • All wiring and cables need to be neat and secured.
  • Wall anchors must be used for all hollow wall installations.

When Rack Mounting CPE

  • Be sure to follow guidance on rack positioning and required space (e.g. rack units required).
  • CPE must be secured properly in the rack.
  • All wiring and cables need to be routed properly, secured, and labeled.
