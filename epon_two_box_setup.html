Overview & Architecture

This guide provides a comprehensive technical overview for the enhanced Fiber to the Premise (FTTP) service using an EPON with a two-box device solution. This solution replaces the XF3 and consists of an Ethernet EPON Optical Node Unit (ONU) paired with either a Technicolor XB7 or a Sercomm XER10 router.

Device Configurations

Optical Node Unit (ONU)

  • Nokia XF-ONU: Model NKFN11AEL (Non-BEAD locations)
  • Sagemcom XF-ONU2: Model SGFN11BEL (All locations)
  • Note: The boot file resides on the ONU, not the gateway.

Technicolor XB7

  • Model: CGM4331COM / CGM433COM
  • Connection: Use Port 4 (2.5G WAN/LAN identified by orange marker) to connect to the ONU.
  • Voice: Required if the customer has Xfinity Voice service.

Sercomm XER10

  • Model: SCER11BEL (WiFi 7 Tri-band)
  • Connection: Use the dedicated WAN port (bottom port).
  • Note: Voice services are not supported on XER10 at launch. Speeds >1Gig require CAT6 (< 180 ft).

Installation Alerts & Reminders

Inspect Before You Connect

Ensure the connector and ONU port are clean, undamaged, and free of debris before provisioning.

SC/UPC (Blue)

Flat polish. This is the required connector for the ONU.

SC/APC (Green)

Angled polish. DO NOT install directly into the ONU. Use a hybrid jumper/coupler if present.

Do Not Tamper Tags

Technicians must attach a blue "Do Not Tamper" tag to the fiber cable at the ONU to prevent customers from unplugging or damaging the connection.

  • Text: "Property of Comcast. Do not tamper with connections or remove devices."
  • Placement: On the fiber connector boot.
  • Visibility: Ensure the tag faces outward inside a media distribution panel.

Activation Steps via Tech360

1

Power Up the ONU

Connect the fiber drop securely (listen for the click). Power up the ONU and wait for the registration process to complete (all three LEDs should be solid green).

2

Add Devices in Tech360

Ensure the ONU and the appropriate gateway (XB7 or XER10) are listed under "Devices to Install". Use the ONU serial number to add it if not present.

3

Connect the Ethernet

Connect the gateway and ONU with a CAT5e/CAT6 Ethernet cable. (Port 4 for XB7, WAN port for XER10).

4

Activate Services

Initiate the startup sequence in Tech360. Note: Be patient. The XB7 may take up to 7-10 minutes to come online (blinking amber to solid white). Do not power cycle during this process.

Troubleshooting & LED Meanings

ONU LED States

Power PON Online Status
Green Green Green Ready. Registered with OLT.
Green Green Blinking Code download underway.
Green Off Off No light from OLT. Check fiber.
Green Green Off / Blink IP connectivity to OLT interrupted.

XER10 Flashing Amber

The device is trying to register. Potential causes: ONU LEDs not locked on green, incorrect firmware on XER10, bad Ethernet cable, or connected to the wrong port.

XER10 Solid Red

Indicates no internet connectivity. Verify the ONU LEDs are solid green. Test the fiber connector (clean SC/UPC) and ensure the Ethernet is seated properly with a click. Factory reset if necessary.

Reboot Sequences

When swapping devices, always reboot in sequence. The ONU may hold the old router's IP address; rebooting the ONU before connecting the new router should clear it.
