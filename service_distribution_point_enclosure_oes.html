Service Distribution Point & Enclosure

At a Glance

This OES covers all requirements for the service distribution point and the proper installation of cables and hardware. The terms "cable", "cables", or "drop" may refer to either a coaxial or a fiber drop based on the specification.

  • Boundary: The service distribution point, typically outside the premise, establishes the boundary between the outside network drop cable and the internal premise distribution wiring.
  • Grounding proximity: It must be located within 20 ft of and with access to the power grounding electrode. (Contact supervisor for alternate location approvals).

General Guidelines

Location & Mounting

  • Must be installed in a location easily accessible by technicians.
  • Attach securely to a wall outside the house using appropriate materials.
  • Mounting height: Minimum 4 feet above the ground, not to exceed 5 feet.
  • Clearance: Minimum 1 foot from the utility meter and breaker box.

Cable Entry & Security

  • Bottom Entry Only: All cables and wires must enter the service distribution enclosure from the bottom.
  • Cables must not run through the lid or door of the enclosure.
  • The enclosure door must be properly secured closed.

Mobile Home Installations

For mobile home installations, the service distribution enclosure must be mounted to the service pole or a separate post. It shall never be attached directly to a mobile-type dwelling.

Coaxial Cable with an Enclosure

All new installations must have a service distribution enclosure installed on the side of the premise.

  • Must contain, at a minimum, the bond block plus any service-specific filters.
  • May contain splitter or distribution hardware if feeding multiple outlets from outside.
  • All devices must be secured and attached inside the enclosure.
  • Service Loop: When cutting cables, leave enough slack without exceeding a 270-degree service loop, maintaining the minimum bending radius for the drop cable and bonding wire.

RFOG with an Enclosure

  • Will support an NID/NIU, bondblock, MoCA filter, and any RF distribution hardware needed (e.g., splitters).
  • All excess fiber must be secured and stored within the enclosure.
  • Excess fiber will not be stored at the pole or at the tap in aerial installations. (Stored at the underground ped/vault location in underground installations where designed).

EPON with an Enclosure

  • The enclosure will support an optical bulkhead.
  • Connections will be SC/UPC and used as a demarcation point between the network and the premise.
  • All excess fiber must be stored at the premise enclosure location, not on the pole or at the pedestal/vault.

Existing Coaxial Cable (No Enclosure)

Upgrade Requirements

An existing drop must be upgraded to an enclosure when:

  • Performing a connect / reconnect.
  • There are splitters or amplifiers at the service distribution location.
  • The drop is being replaced.

Note: All prior holes no longer used shall be sealed with RTV or silicone.

Bend Radius & Loop Requirements

When there is no enclosure, the drop cable must be routed and attached to maintain the minimum bend radius of the cable at all times.

  • Series-6 Cable: Minimum 5-inch diameter loops (2½" radius).
  • Series-11 Cable: Minimum 8-inch diameter loops (4½" radius).
  • Loops must be formed in the cable path before attaching the cable to the premise wall at the bondblock location.
  • Loops must not exceed 270 degrees.

DO NOT PUT CIRCLES IN DROP CABLES

Never place full 360-degree loops anywhere along the routing of the drop cable. Max loop allowance is 270 degrees.
